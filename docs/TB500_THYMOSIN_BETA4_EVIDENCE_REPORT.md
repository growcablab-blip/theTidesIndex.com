# THYMOSIN BETA-4 AND TB-500

Two names that most sources treat as one compound. The evidence says they are two
molecules, and the difference decides which human evidence applies to what.

Neither record is published. Neither has been reviewed by a person.
Records: `/peptides/thymosin-beta-4`, `/peptides/tb-500`.
Screenshots: `review/tb500-v1/`. Reference sheets: `build/publications/`.

---

## 1 · What is Thymosin beta-4?

A 43-residue, N-terminally acetylated peptide the human body already makes:

```
Ac-SDKPDMAEIEKFDKSKLKKTETQEKNPLPSKETIEQEKQAGES      MW ≈ 4963 Da
                  └──┬──┘
               17  LKKTETQ  23
```

It is present in almost every cell except erythrocytes, and is the principal
G-actin-sequestering peptide in mammalian cells — one of sixteen beta-thymosins,
three of which occur in humans.

The molecular weight the practitioner handbook prints (4963.4408) agrees with the
average mass computed from the sequence it prints (4963.5).

## 2 · What is TB-500?

**Ac-LKKTETQ** — residues 17 to 23 of thymosin beta-4, N-terminally acetylated.
Seven residues. About 889 Da, computed from the sequence; no source held here
states it. The parent is about 5.6 times the mass.

It is the actin-binding region of the parent peptide, synthesised on its own and
sold under a product name.

## 3 · Are they chemically identical?

**No.** Established analytically, twice, independently:

| Source | Country | Method | Finding |
|---|---|---|---|
| PMID 22962027 (2012) | Italy | HPLC / high-resolution MS of the TB-500 formulation, then synthesis | Ac-LKKTETQ identified in TB-500 |
| PMID 23084823 (2012) | Hong Kong | Equine doping-control LC-MS | "The key ingredient of TB-500 is the peptide LKKTETQ with artificial acetylation of the N-terminus" |

Corroborated by two later papers stating the same sequence (South Korea 2024,
China 2025).

Of the brief's three options, the answer is **B, with a second problem stacked on
it**:

- **B — a defined fragment.** Settled on the chemistry.
- **And C, in commerce.** A 2023 French analysis of products sold online
  (PMID 36482504) found that "the content of TB500/TB1000 products is not
  systematically consistent with its former descriptions". Knowing what the name
  means does not tell anybody what is in a vial.

### The sources, name by name

| Source | Name used | Says it is |
|---|---|---|
| Mass spectrometry, two labs | TB-500 | **7-residue fragment** — measured |
| Campbell, *Optimize Your Health*, p. 190 | TB-500 | **The active region of** the 43-aa peptide |
| Hack Smith, *Complete Guide*, file p. 110 | TB-500 (Thymosin Beta-4 Fragment) | **A fragment** |
| Seeds, *Peptide Protocols*, file p. 177 | TB4 (Thymosin Beta 4) | The parent peptide; never uses "TB-500" |
| LaValle, *Peptide Handbook*, p. 152 | Thymosin beta 4 / Tb4 / **TB-500** | **The same as the 43-residue peptide** — prints the full sequence beneath the name list |
| Rat Achilles study, PMID 42542926 (2026) | TB-500 | **"Synthetic thymosin beta-4"** — in the title |

So the conflation is not "the practitioner literature". It is one practitioner
handbook and one 2026 peer-reviewed paper. Two practitioner sources get it right.

LaValle's own page contains both halves of the answer: the name list that includes
TB-500, and a printed sequence whose residues 17–23 read LKKTETQ.

**Verification issue V-001** is resolved on the chemistry and left open on the
product question.

**Structural decision:** two compound records. Evidence cannot cross between
them, and a test enforces it (see §13 and D-01 in `OWNER_DECISIONS_REQUIRED.md`).

## 4 · What human evidence exists?

### Thymosin beta-4 — seven records, six programmes

Stratified PubMed screen, 13 September 2026: 1,112 records; the 288-record human
stratum classified in full, 26 by hand.

| PMID | Year | Country | Design | n | Route | Finding |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 20536472 | 2010 | United States | Phase I, randomised, placebo-controlled, single + 14-day multiple dose | 40 | IV | No dose-limiting toxicity |
| 34346165 | 2021 | China | First-in-human phase I, randomised, double-blind, recombinant (NL005) | 84 | IV | No DLT, no SAE, no accumulation |
| 25826322 | 2015 | United States | Phase 2 RCT, double-masked, placebo | 9 | Eye drops 0.1% | Discomfort and staining improved at day 56 |
| 20536470 | 2010 | Italy, Poland | Phase 2 RCT, dose-escalation, placebo | 73 | Topical | Safety ≈ placebo; 0.03% "may" accelerate healing |
| 17495250 | 2007 | Italy, Poland | Design + interim report of the same programme | — | Topical | — |
| 34318587 | 2021 | China | Active comparator vs 2% ketoconazole | 71 | Topical gel | Greater efficacy than comparator reported |
| 20457990 | 2010 | United States | Case reports, neurotrophic corneal defects | — | Ophthalmic | No abstract on record |

**The dominant confound:** 603 of the 1,112 records carry the MeSH heading
*Humans* because the body makes this peptide. Seventy-four records in the stratum
measure it as a biomarker. None of those is human evidence of administering it,
and a screen that read the MeSH heading would have inverted the picture.

Also excluded, and kept in the ledger: a Chinese pilot in which Tβ4 pre-treated
patients' own cells in culture before transplantation (ex vivo, not
administration); a 2012 *proposal* for a paediatric cardiac-surgery trial,
outcome unknown.

### TB-500 — none

Census of all 31 PubMed records, every one adjudicated by hand. **No study in which
TB-500 was given to a person.** A 2024 metabolism paper states that "the biological
effects of TB-500 have not been documented".

**Eight of the 31 records are false string matches** — a broiler feed additive code,
a tuberculosis case count ("500 had TB"), an antipsychotic tablet price table, an
arrhythmogenic dose, biochar, a plant extract, theobromine, an HIV survey. That is
26% of the result count.

## 5 · What preclinical evidence exists?

**Thymosin beta-4:** 107 animal, 4 ex vivo and 9 in vitro records within the
human stratum alone. The universe beyond the stratum (824 records) is characterised
by counts and not individually classified: 1,001 carry the MeSH heading *Animals*,
306 cell-culture or in-vitro headings.

**TB-500:** 6 animal, 2 in vitro, 9 analytical methods. Of note —

- Rat Achilles transection, 32 animals, intraperitoneal, 4 weeks: higher load to
  failure, lower Bonar and Movin scores in the TB-500 arm. The paper misnames the
  compound in its own title.
- Rat metabolism: Ac-LK the principal early metabolite, Ac-LKK detected to 72 h;
  in fibroblasts only the metabolite **Ac-LKKTE** showed wound-healing activity, not
  the parent. What is administered and what acts may be different molecules.
- A corneal-repair hydrogel in human corneal cells and a rat alkali burn.

## 6 · What findings have independent replication?

| Finding | State | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Tβ4 is tolerated IV by healthy volunteers | **Independent, two countries** | US 2010 and China 2021: no shared group, decade, or preparation |
| TB-500 is Ac-LKKTETQ | **Independent, four countries** | Italy, Hong Kong, South Korea, China |
| Topical Tβ4 accelerates venous ulcer healing | **Repeated, same group** | Two papers, one Rome programme |
| Tβ4 eye drops improve dry eye | Single study | n = 9 |
| rhTβ4 gel improves seborrheic dermatitis | Single study | n = 71, active comparator |
| TB-500 improves tendon healing | Single study | Rats; compound identity uncertain |
| Tβ4 improves musculoskeletal injury in people | **Nothing found** | The first use practitioners list |
| TB-500 has any effect in a person | **Nothing found** | — |

The strongest replicated *biological* finding on either record is a safety finding.
No efficacy result for either compound has been reproduced by an independent group.

Replication caveat recorded on the safety finding: the two phase I dose ranges sit
roughly five orders of magnitude apart and neither abstract explains why.

which it is used.

## 10 · What meaningful research gaps remain?

**Thymosin beta-4** (9): subcutaneous route; the five-order dose gap between phase I
programmes; independent replication of any efficacy result; any musculoskeletal
human study; safety beyond ~3 months; product content; full texts and the 824
unclassified records; tumour-tissue expression; regulatory position.

**TB-500** (8): any human effect; human safety; product content; whether any parent
finding transfers to the fragment; any route with PK; parent vs metabolite
activity; validation of any reported regimen; regulatory position.

## 11 · What research questions emerge?

Derived from the gaps above, as stored on the records:

**Identity** — Does Ac-LKKTETQ reproduce the effects of full-length Tβ4 in a
comparable model, and at what relative exposure?

**Route** — What are the PK of subcutaneous Tβ4 in humans compared with IV? Does
intact Ac-LKKTETQ reach the circulation after subcutaneous injection?

**Dose–response** — What accounts for the dose difference between the synthetic and
recombinant phase I programmes?

**Independent replication** — Can the dry-eye, venous-ulcer or dermatitis result be
reproduced by another group?

**Human evidence** — Does Tβ4 affect musculoskeletal injury healing in humans by a
route with established PK? Does Ac-LKKTETQ have any measurable effect in humans?

**Safety** — Tβ4 beyond three months, in patients. Ac-LKKTETQ at all.

**Mechanism** — Is TB-500's activity the parent's or a metabolite's? Is elevated Tβ4
in tumour tissue a cause, consequence or marker?

**Product characterisation** — How consistently do products sold under either name
contain what they claim, across suppliers and batches?

**Protocol validation** — What exposure do the regimens practitioners report for
TB-500 produce, and is any of them tolerated?

## 12 · What limited regulatory context is worth retaining?

Very little, and it is kept small on purpose.

- **Thymosin beta-4:** recorded as *investigational* on the strength of the phase I
  and phase 2 studies. No regulator's own record consulted. No approval, refusal or
  authorisation held in any jurisdiction.
- **TB-500:** recorded as *not approved*, on a researcher's statement in a 2023
  analytical paper. Not a regulator's record.

Neither status appears above the evidence on either page, and neither is phrased as
a judgment on the science. Thymosin beta-4 is the clearest case in the register of
why that matters: no approval anywhere, and more replicated human safety data than
the approved compound's off-label uses.
