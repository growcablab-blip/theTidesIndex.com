# The Tides Index

**Domain:** thetidesindex.com  
**Working tagline:** Independent peptide science & clinical reference.

The Tides Index is an independent peptide education, evidence, quality, and professional-reference platform designed for two audiences:

1. **Patients / general readers** who need clear, visual, non-prescriptive education.
2. **Practitioners / clinic staff** who need traceable evidence, mechanisms, source-specific protocols, administration-route context, quality/testing guidance, and direct provenance.

This repository is the product foundation. The core asset is **not the website and not the PDFs**. The core asset is a structured evidence system that can publish multiple experiences from the same reviewed data.

## Non-negotiable principles

- No Velara branding or commercial cross-promotion.
- Source PDFs are private research inputs. Do not publish or redistribute copyrighted books unless separately licensed.
- Do not convert practitioner opinion into clinical fact.
- Do not merge conflicting protocols into an invented “standard protocol.”
- Separate source type, evidence type, verification status, and regulatory status.
- Patient mode must not function as a self-dosing guide.
- Practitioner protocol records must retain source, context, route, amount reported, frequency, duration, population/model, monitoring, cautions, and evidence status.
- Every meaningful published claim must be traceable to a source location.
- All pages must support revision, review dates, and corrections.
